# Lottus Services Inc. — 2026 Digital Rebrand

The root website now presents Lottus as a **Digital Systems & Web Development** company rather than a hauling / dump-trailer operation.

## What changed
- Replaced the former hauling, equipment, landscaping and jobsite-support homepage.
- New corporate technology positioning with a modern responsive design.
- Added clear capability areas: web experiences, business portals, operational systems, integrations and automation.
- Added platform/system portfolio: PLEASE, CAL, Integrity Concrete Solutions and KineticaPlus.
