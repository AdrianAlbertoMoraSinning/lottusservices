Frequently asked questions

What to clarify before we scope a project.

Can a project start with only a website?

Yes. A public website can be delivered as a focused project and later connect to booking, accounts, payments, portals or internal workflows.

Can Lottus work with an existing process or system?

Yes. Discovery begins with the workflow you already use so we can identify what should remain, change or integrate.

Who owns access to the deployed system?

Hosting, access, data custody, licensing and handoff are defined in the project scope before launch.

Are integrations included automatically?

No. Payments, email, APIs and third-party services are included when they are part of the agreed scope and supported by the selected provider.

What happens after launch?

Support, maintenance and future improvements are agreed as part of the project rather than assumed to be unlimited.
