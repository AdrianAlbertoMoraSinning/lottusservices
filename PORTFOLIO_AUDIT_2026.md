# Lottus Services Inc. — Portfolio & Positioning Audit (September 2026)

## Why the former homepage no longer fit
The root website was still positioned around hauling, dump-trailer work, landscaping and jobsite support. That message no longer represented the direction or the strongest body of work in the repository.

## What the repository demonstrates today
The current codebase contains substantially more than marketing websites. It includes examples of:

- service-operation portals and customer/provider workflows (`please/`)
- restaurant reservations, ordering, commerce, staff tools and Supabase workflows (`SumaQ17th/`)
- moving/logistics websites with booking, invoice, Stripe and Google review integrations (`rbmoving/`, `GroupeVGC/`)
- retail / digital showroom concepts (`Onta/`, `LuBoutique/`)
- professional-service and technical profile experiences (`WellOilandGas/`)
- local-service portfolio and payment experiences (`JPPaintings/`)
- a broader web-development platform/tooling initiative (`Octon-WebDev/`)
- serverless Netlify functions and payment/database integrations at repository level.

Outside the folders in this particular ZIP, the Lottus portfolio also includes active work such as CAL, Concrete Operations & Quality System, KineticaPlus, Montecristo Auto Finance and Strata Drilling. The new homepage references the strongest of those as portfolio/system work without creating broken local routes.

## New corporate position
**Lottus Services Inc. — Digital Systems & Web Development**

The new root presentation is deliberately broader than “web design.” It is organized around four commercial capabilities:

1. Websites & Digital Experiences
2. Business Portals
3. Operational Systems
4. Integrations & Automation

This supports both simple client websites and the more valuable custom-system work Lottus is already producing.

## Credibility strategy used in the redesign
Instead of generic claims, the page now shows two proof layers:

- **Platforms & systems:** PLEASE, CAL, Concrete Operations & Quality System and KineticaPlus.
- **Selected work:** SumaQ on 17th, Groupe VGC, RB Moving, ONTA, JP Painting and Drilling & Well Engineering, plus secondary portfolio references.

## Technical / deployment decisions
- Existing project directories were preserved.
- Existing Stripe checkout backend contract was preserved.
- The payment and success pages were restyled to the new corporate identity.
- The former `/webdev/` page now redirects to the new main capabilities section so outdated package/pricing content is not presented as the primary offering.
- Legacy truck/trailer/landscaping assets used only by the retired homepage were removed from this delivery.
- Root metadata and the manifest now reflect the technology business.

## Suggested next iteration after live review
After the new root site is deployed and visually reviewed on the real domain, the highest-value next improvements would be to add polished screenshots/case-study details for CAL, Concrete Operations & Quality System and KineticaPlus, and connect a dedicated lead-intake workflow or CRM-backed project inquiry form if Lottus wants to move beyond WhatsApp/phone as the primary contact path.
