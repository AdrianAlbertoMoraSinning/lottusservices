01
Service operations · Flagship platform

PLEASE

Live deployment

A complete service-orchestration platform connecting the customer request with the internal team, service providers, operational execution, billing and reporting.

What has been built

  • Public service catalogue, quote and service-request flows.
  • Administration, developer provisioning and provider portals with separate roles and sessions.
  • Request → Job conversion, master calendar, provider assignment/reassignment and multi-provider teams.
  • Customer tracking, scheduling changes, operational status and photo/document workflows.
  • Invoices, Stripe-ready checkout, provider payments, revenue/cost/gross-profit separation and reports.
  • Transactional notifications and operational exports.

Technical strength

PLEASE demonstrates the highest level of workflow complexity in the portfolio. The data model preserves lifecycle history while allowing controlled edits, and the portal separates public, customer, provider, admin and developer concerns rather than forcing everything into one interface.

What this can become

The architecture can be adapted to field service, staffing, dispatch, home services, trades, facilities, contractor networks or any operation where jobs move through multiple people and states.

Industrial operations · Concrete production

Integrity Concrete Solutions

Client portal

An operations portal that joins quality-control inputs with production and inventory instead of treating them as separate spreadsheets.

What has been built

  • Administrator and Operator role profiles.
  • Aggregate moisture tests and weather conditions.
  • Concrete cylinder strength tracking and formula catalogue.
  • Batch operations, production posting and raw-material consumption.
  • Unified inventory, Kardex ledger and controlled adjustments.
  • Dashboards, audit trail and in-app role-aware operations manual.

Technical strength

The portal translates a real industrial workflow into linked records: quality inputs influence water adjustments; production consumes inventory; Kardex preserves movement history; roles and RLS control access.

What this can become

Scheduling, maintenance, purchasing, cost-per-batch, equipment uptime, supplier performance and BI dashboards can be added without discarding the existing operational foundation.

03
Professional practice · Practitioner platform

KineticaPlus

Client platform

A practice-management system derived from proven scheduling and provider architecture, then adapted to practitioners, rooms, insurance/cash workflows and clinical documentation.

What has been built

  • Administration, practitioner and developer-provisioning portals.
  • Requests, appointments, master calendar and practitioner assignment.
  • Room rental workflows and rental administration.
  • Invoices, practitioner payments, insurance/cash ledger.
  • Clinical SOAP notes with Draft → Signed → Amended lifecycle.
  • Audit logs, private practitioner documents and role-based clinical access.

Technical strength

The important engineering result is adaptation: generic field-service concepts were refactored into domain-specific clinical language and permissions, while signed notes become read-only and later changes are appended as amendments rather than silently overwritten.

What this can become

With privacy/security review, the platform can continue toward deeper patient intake, insurer integrations, payments, room utilization, practitioner analytics and external scheduling/payment APIs.

04
Business finance · Accounting architecture

CAL 1.5

Product / demo

Canadian Accounting by Lottus is designed as a client-isolated accounting platform rather than a shared multi-customer data pool.

What has been built

  • Financial dashboard, receivables and payables foundation.
  • Banking/reconciliation workspace and general journal controls.
  • GST/HST/QST working papers, financial reports and exports.
  • Canadian starter chart of accounts and audit trail.
  • Private document-vault architecture and legal acceptance workflow.
  • Customer-controlled Supabase deployment model.

Technical strength

CAL demonstrates architecture choices driven by data custody. Production customers are intended to receive separate controlled database/storage environments, with sensitive writes mediated server-side and posted accounting events handled by reversal/adjustment instead of silent deletion.

What this can become

Portal integrations can feed approved invoices and operational events into CAL while maintaining accounting separation; future modules can add richer reconciliation, budgeting and management reporting.

Consumer SaaS · Sports social product

PoolKick

Product prototype

A responsive tournament prediction platform that turns private football pools into a complete user product with scoring, competition and administration.

06
Developer tooling · Verification

Octon v1.5

Engineering tool

A portal review engine that separates suspected static-code problems from production-verified failures before scoring a deployment.

What has been built

  • Repository map and code-health pipeline.
  • Production verification of assets, routes, favicons and Netlify Functions.
  • Confidence states: confirmed, probable, needs verification, verified in production.
  • False-positive clearing when production proves a target exists.
  • Read-only GitHub App access, SSRF protections and credential redaction.
  • Traceable evidence with target, status and verification timestamp.

Technical strength

Octon proves capability in developer tooling and defensive automation. It intentionally minimizes automatic write authority and treats ambiguous evidence as inconclusive instead of manufacturing certainty.

What this can become

Continuous quality gates, deployment checks, regression monitoring, client-site health dashboards and approval-controlled remediation workflows.
