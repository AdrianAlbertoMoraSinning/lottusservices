Principle 01

Human authority

Octon recommends and prepares. Repository writes require an explicit, specific approval.

Principle 02

Least privilege

GitHub credentials should be fine-grained and limited to repositories Octon is allowed to manage.

Principle 03

Evidence before change

Every proposal should include why the change matters, expected impact, risk, test plan and rollback path.

Principle 04

Client separation

Portal-specific credentials and client data remain segregated. Octon stores control metadata, not pooled client operational data.
