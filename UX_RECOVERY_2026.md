# Lottus UX Recovery — September 2026

This iteration restores the creative/motion layer without replacing real product evidence.

- The steel intro is only shown on the Home initial entry.
- Contact and every other menu/action use only the short metallic click sound.
- The click sound is intentionally short and distinct from the full intro sound.
- Manual “Load live preview” placeholders were removed.
- Real project iframes now load directly (browser-native lazy loading except the Home hero, which loads eagerly).
- Case studies show one large interface preview instead of repeating three nearly identical frames.
- Selected motion pieces are retained as a supporting explanatory layer on Home, Portfolio, capability pages and flagship cases.
- External project frames retain an Open ↗ route for browsers/sites that prohibit embedding.
